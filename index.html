Southern Night Sky

Naos
Blue supergiant ~1,000 light-years away. One of the most luminous known stars in Puppis.
Suhail
Ancient southern navigation star used by Arabian and maritime explorers.
Tureis
Yellow-white giant star visible in southern skies during summer.
Betelgeuse
Red supergiant nearing the end of its life. Would engulf inner planets if placed in our Solar System.
Rigel
Extremely luminous blue supergiant ~860 light-years away.
Alnilam
Central belt star of Orion, far more massive than the Sun.
Acrux
Brightest star in the Southern Cross system. Actually a multi-star system of hot blue stars.
Mimosa
Hot blue giant with intense ultraviolet output.
Sirius
Brightest star in the night sky. A binary system just 8.6 light-years away.
Adhara
Massive blue-white star once among the brightest in Earth’s sky.
