Southern Night Sky

Naos
A luminous blue supergiant approximately 1,090 light-years from Earth. One of the brightest stars in Puppis and over 700,000 times more luminous than the Sun.
Suhail
Historically used by navigators across the southern oceans. Its Arabic name refers to brightness and smoothness in the night sky.
Tureis
A giant yellow-white star in Puppis, visible to the naked eye from the southern hemisphere during summer months.
Betelgeuse
A massive red supergiant nearing the end of its life cycle. If placed at the center of our Solar System, it would extend beyond Jupiter’s orbit.
Rigel
A brilliant blue-white supergiant roughly 860 light-years away. It radiates tens of thousands of times more energy than the Sun.
Alnilam
The central star in Orion’s Belt. Extremely luminous and visible across vast interstellar distances.
Acrux
The brightest star in the Southern Cross. Actually a multiple star system composed of intensely hot blue stars.
Mimosa
A hot blue giant star with rapid rotation and strong ultraviolet radiation emissions.
Sirius
The brightest star visible in Earth’s night sky. Located only 8.6 light-years away and part of a binary star system.
Adhara
An enormous blue-white giant star once considered among the brightest stars visible from Earth thousands of years ago.
